Privacy

Last updated November 28, 2026. Short version: search happens on your Mac, files you send go straight to the other Mac, and we keep only what's needed to connect people.

Search stays on your Mac

Rover's index of your files, the text it reads in your images, your drive catalogs and your pinned files are stored only on your Mac. They are never uploaded.

The assistant (optional)

If you use the assistant (⇥ Tab), your question and the file names and contents it looks at are sent to Anthropic's Claude through your own Claude login, under Anthropic's terms. Nothing is sent unless you ask it something.

Sending files (Rover Pro)

  • Files go directly from the sender's Mac to the receiver's Mac, encrypted end to end. We never store them. When two Macs can't connect directly, encrypted traffic passes through our relay, which can't read it.
  • Your account: when you sign in with Google we receive your email address, name and profile picture, and keep them to show who sent what.
  • Your Macs: each Mac gets a random device ID (a public key) so people can send to it.
  • Offers: when you send to someone, we store the sender, the recipients' email addresses, the file names, sizes and your note so the recipient can accept it. Offers are deleted after 7 days (30 days for your sent list).
  • Gmail contacts (only if you choose to import them) are read on your Mac and stay there. We never receive them.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see your card number. We receive your subscription status and email address from Stripe, and Rover checks about once a month that your subscription is active.

The website

getrover.dev sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Where data lives

Account and offer data is stored on Amazon Web Services (US East). The website runs on Vercel. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Your choices

Sign out from Rover's menu to end your session and revoke Rover's access to your Google account. To delete your account and everything stored for it, email hello@getrover.dev, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll say so on this page and in Rover's release notes.
