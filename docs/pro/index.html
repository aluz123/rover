How it works

After you subscribe, you land on a page with your key and an Open in Rover button. One click and Pro is on. You can also paste the key in Rover's menu → Get Rover Pro. Rover renews the key by itself each month while you're subscribed.

Lost your key? Open the receipt link again: it shows a working key for as long as you're subscribed.

Cancel anytime from Rover's menu → Manage Subscription. Pro keeps working until the end of the month you paid for.

What leaves my Mac? Search stays on your Mac: images are read by Apple's Vision framework and drive catalogs are stored locally. Files you send go straight to the other Mac, encrypted end to end; our servers only help the two Macs find each other. Once a month Rover checks with getrover.dev that your subscription is still active.

Which images? Screenshots, downloads and pictures in your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Pictures and iCloud Drive folders. Rover reads the newest first, a few per second, in the background. Photos inside the Photos app aren't included.

Is Pro open source? Rover's search is, and stays free under the MIT License. Rover Pro is closed source and comes only in the official app from getrover.dev. Your subscription pays for the servers that make sending fast and keeps Rover maintained.

Refunds: payments are final, so there are no refunds, including for part of a month. Cancel anytime to stop the next charge. If a key doesn't work, open the receipt link again or open an issue.
